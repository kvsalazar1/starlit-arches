It runs on your computer.

The calendar widgets run in Rainmeter. A Windows helper connects directly to Google with your permission, then saves a local snapshot for the desktop views. You choose which calendars appear in each widget.

Sign-in opens Google's page in your browser. The utility requests permission to read calendar events and your subscribed-calendar list. It has no function to create, edit, or delete your events.

Read-only access

Your calendar stays under your control. Display filters do not change Google Calendar.

Local storage

Windows protects the saved connection. Event data is cached locally for the views.

No app account

There is no separate Starlit Arches account, calendar server, advertising, or analytics service.

A small, personal utility.

Starlit Arches is a personal Windows desktop project, rather than a hosted calendar service. This information site explains the calendar connection; it does not sign you in or receive calendar events.

Review the privacy policy for data categories, storage, retention, and disconnecting. The terms describe the utility's intended use and practical limits.
