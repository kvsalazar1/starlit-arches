Terms of use

Effective October 22, 2026

Starlit Arches is a personal Windows utility for displaying Google Calendar information in Rainmeter. These terms describe its intended use and limitations.

Purpose and permission

Use the calendar feature with a Google account and calendars you are authorized to access. Connecting the utility is optional. You control the Google authorization and can remove it at any time.

The utility requests read-only access to calendar events and the subscribed-calendar list. It does not create, edit, or delete events. Calendar visibility choices affect the desktop widgets only.

Your data and settings

The privacy policy explains data categories, local storage, retention, and disconnection. You remain responsible for access to your Windows account, local files, backups, and any sync services you configure.

Unloading a widget stops its automatic polling but does not by itself revoke Google access or delete the saved files. Follow the privacy policy's disconnect steps to remove both access and local data.

Accuracy and availability

The widgets are a convenient view of your calendar. Refreshes can be delayed or fail, and an offline snapshot may be older than Google Calendar. Check the status shown by the widget and use Google Calendar to confirm important times or changes.

This personal utility is provided as available, without a promise of uninterrupted service, permanent authorization, or ongoing support. Google permissions, policies, and service availability can affect the connection.

Independent project

Starlit Arches is not an official Google or Rainmeter product and is not endorsed or verified by them. Google and Rainmeter names identify the services and software with which the utility works. Google's services remain subject to Google's own terms and policies.

This information site does not offer a separate Starlit Arches account or hosted calendar service.

Stopping use

You can unload or remove the calendar skins whenever you wish. Revoke the application's Google Account connection and delete its local cache and connection files if you want to fully disconnect. See the privacy policy for the exact steps.
